Metric definitions & source notes How to read this dashboard

Time to fill

Calendar days from Posting Date to the recorded completion date for Completed rows with valid dates. One Completed row explicitly documented as a removed posting (ID 1740559) is excluded. This is a completion-based proxy, not verified offer acceptance; internal role coverage is included. Candidate start dates are not used.

Records & monthly filters

Each worksheet row is a posting episode, not a unique hire. The file contains 33 records and 32 unique posting IDs; ID 1835245 has two distinct posting dates and both are retained. Posting-month filters select a cohort; monthly charts then show that cohort’s posting, completion, or sponsorship events in their actual months.

Sponsorship amounts

Only numeric amounts and parsable currency entries are summed. Blank or N/A amounts are not assumed to be zero. Six of 22 sponsored records lack amounts, and seven records have no sponsorship status. Recorded amounts are not independently verified expenses. Two sponsorship dates occur after completion and are retained as entered.

Role & location comparisons

Center Director variants, Intake Coordinator variants, Case Manager variants, and RBT / Registered Behavior Technician are grouped into role families. Lead RBT stays separate. Comparisons show eligible sample counts; one-record results offer limited evidence. A city entered as “California” is shown as “Not specified.”

Historical status

No records are marked Open. Two are On Hold, and 11 are Removed. Days to outcome ends at the recorded completion, removal, or hold date; it is not current open age. Two Removed records have no outcome date. The workbook does not provide a current active-opening inventory.
